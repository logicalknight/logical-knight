01Why Agent Research

          
“Page changed” is where the work begins.

          
Page monitoring tells you that something moved. Agent Research tells you what happened, who it affects and where the proof is — in a form your team and your software can use.

        

        

          

            Simple page monitoring
            

              Page changed
              acme.example/en/sites · 14 lines
              <div class="hero-banner v2">
              <div class="hero-banner v3">
              <p>Our new Bremen facility will…
              © 2025 Acme Example Industries
              © 2026 Acme Example Industries
            

            
Was it the footer, a layout tweak, or a new facility? Someone still has to read it, decide and write it up.

08Product principles

          
Product principles

        

        

          
P.01
Evidence first.
Every useful event should point back to exactly what changed.

          
P.02
Noise matters.
A monitoring system that sends everything is not intelligence.

          
P.03
Built for systems.
Events should be useful to both humans and software.

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          
09What exists today

          
We’re early. Here’s what Agent Research does.

          
No borrowed logos or inflated numbers — just the capabilities the product has today.

        

        

          
01
Plain-English watches and research plans
Describe a business question; review the proposed plan and its gaps before anything runs.
Available in the free beta

          
02
Verification requirements and coverage
You set what counts as verified; results say when coverage was incomplete.
Available in the free beta

          
03
Evidence-backed, multilingual events
Original text, source and capture time in six languages; fact kept apart from inference.
Available

          
04
API, webhooks and briefings
Pull results, receive signed webhooks, or read daily and weekly briefings.
Available in the free beta

          
05
Machine-to-machine payment
Paying per result with x402, for software agents.
In development

10Our approach

            
We start with the problem.

            
Not with a technology looking for a use. Each product begins as a real workflow that is slow, costly or unreliable today.

          

          
    
            
  1. 01
    Find a real problem
    Where existing workflows are manual, fragmented, expensive or inadequate.
    2. 
            
  2. 02
    Understand the workflow
    Learn how the work is actually done before deciding what to build.
    3. 
            
  3. 03
    Build the smallest useful system
    Solve the core problem properly, without unnecessary scope.
    4. 
            
  4. 04
    Validate it
    Test with the people who have the problem, and learn from real use.
    5. 
            
  5. 05
    Turn it into a reliable product
    Harden what works into something people and systems can depend on.
