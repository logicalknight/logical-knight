The problem

  • The proof must fit the wood actually used.

    For pernambuco, proof must relate to the wood in the bow, from raw wood to the finished bow. An invoice alone is often not enough.

  • Books grow over years.

    Boards become blanks, blanks become sticks and bows, and some pieces break. Paper books and spreadsheets lose this chain.

  • Old stock has gaps.

    Historical stock or bought blanks often have no complete history. That must stay visible, not be filled in.

What you do, from stock to handover

  1. 01

    Bring in your stock

    Import your existing inventory from a CSV or Excel file, or enter pieces by hand. You see a preview first; every row is checked; nothing is saved until you confirm.

  2. 02

    Record wood origins

    For each origin: species, where it came from, the harvest period, how you acquired it and the documents you hold. Gaps stay marked as unknown.

  3. 03

    Identify and process pieces

    Give each piece your own code. Split boards into identified blanks, shape them, make bows, and record breakage and waste as you go.

  4. 04

    Attach photographs and documents

    Add photos, invoices, permits and other files to the pieces and origins they belong to.

  5. 05

    Correct with a reason

    If a record is wrong, correct it with a reason. The old and new values stay in the history; stated history such as “historical stock” is never changed silently.

  6. 06

    Review your stock

    See counts, weight and volume as recorded, per kind and status, as an internal report.

  7. 07

    Hand over a selected package

    For a single bow, choose the documents to share and prepare a handover package.

One board, eight blanks: a worked example

A fictional workshop, to show how the records hold together. Nothing here is a real customer.

  1. 01

    A board is recorded

    Pernambuco board BR-2021-003 is entered with its species, its stated origin, the year it was acquired and the invoice and permit the workshop holds. What is not known is left marked as unknown.

  2. 02

    The board becomes eight blanks

    The board is split into eight individually identified blanks, BL-01 to BL-08. Each keeps its own code and points back to the board it came from.

  3. 03

    Processing, breakage and waste

    Five blanks are shaped into bow sticks; one, BL-04, cracks while bending and is recorded as breakage; the offcuts are recorded as waste. Each piece is used only once, so nothing is counted twice.

  4. 04

    A finished bow

    Blank BL-02 becomes finished bow BOW-114, with its photographs and the maker's stamp.

  5. 05

    Select the bow, see its ancestry

    Opening BOW-114 shows the chain back to blank BL-02, to board BR-2021-003 and to the recorded origin, with the documents attached along the way.

What the module does today

  • Wood origins

    Species, origin, harvest period, acquisition and documents. Unknown facts stay marked as unknown.

  • Stock

    Each piece with your own code, an optional stamp or identification mark and photos. Pieces, weight and volume are separate values; nothing is converted.

  • Processing steps

    Split a board into individually identified blanks, shape, make a bow, record breakage and waste. Each piece can be used only once. A wrong step is reversed with a reason, not deleted.

  • Corrections

    Changes need a reason and are kept with old and new values. Stated history such as “historical stock” is never changed silently.

  • Your existing books

    Import a CSV or Excel inventory. You see a preview first; every row is checked; nothing is saved until you confirm.

  • Inventory report

    Counts, weight and volume as recorded, per kind and status. It is an internal report, not an official or regulatory format.

  • Handover

    Per bow: a package with the documents you select, a readable summary and checksums. Private notes and the rest of your stock stay out.

A handover, in practice

What leaves the workshop with a bow, and what stays.

  • You choose the documents

    For bow BOW-114 you select, say, the origin record for board BR-2021-003, the supplier invoice and the import permit. You leave out anything you do not want to share.

  • The package is self-contained

    It holds your selection, a readable summary of the bow and its chain, and checksums so the recipient can check nothing was altered.

  • The rest stays private

    Your private notes, your other stock and your other bows are not in the package.

Questions about wood and workshop records

Can I bring my wood inventory from Excel?

Yes. You can import a CSV or Excel inventory. Every row is checked and shown in a preview. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

How do I keep the connection from a board to a finished bow?

Give each piece your own code and record the processing steps. When you split a board, identify the resulting blanks individually and follow them through the workshop. Breakage and waste are recorded too.

What if my old stock has missing origin records?

Record what you know and attach the documents you have. Unknown facts and gaps in the history stay visible. The module does not reconstruct missing history or certify lawful origin.

Which records can I hand over with a bow?

Choose the documents for that bow. The handover package contains your selection, a readable summary and checksums. Private notes and unrelated stock stay out.

How can my workshop try the module?

Contact us by e-mail to request a pilot with your own records. We discuss your workflow and set up access for your workshop. The module is not generally available; we explain the terms before you commit.

What it does not do

  • It does not certify lawful origin and it does not authenticate a bow.
  • It does not fill in missing history or guess conversions between pieces, weight and volume.
  • It does not replace the authorities, a lawyer or an expert opinion.

Status and access
