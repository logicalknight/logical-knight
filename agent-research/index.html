Turn business questions into continuously verified intelligence.

03Verification

          
You decide what counts as verified.

          
Set the evidence an event needs before it is treated as verified. The same rules apply every time, and every result shows which requirement was met — or missed.

        

        
    
          
  • Official source required
  • Two or more independent sources
  • Evidence freshness
  • Accepted source types
  • What to do when sources disagree
    • 
        

        

          

            No verified change
            

              
Required sources
3 of 3 checked successfully

              
Answer
NO_VERIFIED_CHANGE

            

            
Every source that matters was checked, and none showed a qualifying change.

          

          

            Coverage incomplete
            

              
Required sources
2 of 3 checked · 1 could not be reached

              
Answer
COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE

            

            
Silence is not treated as proof that nothing happened. If an important source could not be checked, Agent Research says so.

          

        

        

          
When sources disagree
Disagreement is kept.
When sources disagree, Agent Research can preserve the disagreement instead of silently presenting one interpretation as established fact.

          
History
Intelligence evolves.
Events move through states — candidate, verified, confirmed, disputed, superseded or retracted — and the history is kept instead of treating every alert as an isolated fact.

          
Example
A price, over time.
€49 → €59 (superseded) → €49 (current)

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          
04Understand

          
Evidence says what happened. Analysis says what it may mean.

          
Recommended actions remain recommendations. The three are labelled separately in every result, so an interpretation is never passed off as a fact.

        

        

          
Observed fact
What the source published.
“Our new Bremen facility will begin operations in the fourth quarter.” — quoted exactly, with source and time.

          
Inference
What it may mean.
Acme Example Industries is opening a facility in Bremen. Labelled as an interpretation of the evidence.

          
Suggested action
What your team might do.
Assess the new site as a sales opportunity in its region. A suggestion — never something the company did.
